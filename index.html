What this platform aims to achieve

Many students cannot visit the physical library every day. This platform brings the library to the students, so they can search and read textbooks from their department at home.

Students can log in with their Library Card Number and read textbooks without coming to the physical library.

Organised by Faculty and Department

Books are arranged exactly like the physical library, by faculty, then Department, then Course.

Trusted Textbooks

Only textbooks used in AE-FUNAI are listed, so students do not waste time on wron materials.

Questions about the project?

Do I need to come to the library to use it?

No You can log in from home with your Library Card Number.

Are the boks the same as in the school library?

Yes. They are arranged by Faculty and Department, just like the physical library.

Do I need to pay to use it?

No. It is free for all AE-FUNAI students with a valid Library Card Number.

Can I read on my phone?

Yes. The website is responsive and works on phone, tablet and laptop.
